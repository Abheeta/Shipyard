# SHIPYARD — USER INTERVIEW GUIDE

Use conversationally. Do not ask every question. Prioritize recent real behavior over opinions about hypothetical products.

## Fitness interview

1. Tell me about the last workout video/Reel you saved.
2. Where did you find it?
3. Why did you save it?
4. What were you hoping to do with it when you saved it?
5. When did you actually use it?
6. Can you show me exactly how you found it again?
7. What happened between finding it and actually starting the workout?
8. Roughly how many workout videos do you think you have saved?
9. How do you currently organize them?
10. Have you ever known that you saved a workout but been unable to find it?
11. How long does it take to find a specific one?
12. What do you do when you cannot find it?
13. What information do you have to extract yourself: exercises, sets, reps, rest, equipment?
14. Do you pause/rewind?
15. Do you write the workout into another app?
16. Do you use a separate fitness app while following the video?
17. Have you tried another solution?
18. What worked and what did not?
19. If Instagram had perfect search across saved posts tomorrow, would that completely solve the problem?
20. What is the most annoying part of your current workflow?

## Broader saved-content discovery

1. What was the last useful thing you saved from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or elsewhere?
2. Why did you save it?
3. Where did you save it?
4. Have you used it?
5. If not, why not?
6. What usually happens after you save something?
7. What happens when you need it later?
8. How do you search for it?
9. Do you forget why you saved things?
10. Do you save the same thing in multiple places?
11. Do you screenshot or send posts to yourself because saving isn't enough?
12. How often do saved items become something you do, buy, cook, learn, visit or create?
13. Would one place for content from multiple platforms be useful?
14. What would make you switch from your current system?

## Exploratory / geeky / B2B

1. What is something you repeatedly have to search, compare, organize, reconcile, copy or check that you hate doing?
2. Tell me about the last time you had to do it.
3. What information did you start with and where did it live?
4. What steps did you take?
5. Which part took longest?
6. Which part was most error-prone?
7. What do you currently use?
8. Spreadsheet, script, checklist, Notion, manual process?
9. Why haven't you replaced the workaround?
10. What happens if you make a mistake?
11. How often does it happen?
12. Have you paid for software or outsourced any part?
13. If a tool could take the raw information and produce the finished result, what would you want it to produce?
14. What would you still want to review yourself?
15. Is there another problem you deal with that is even more painful?
