# SHIPYARD — GOOGLE FORMS CONTENT (3 forms)

Paste these directly into Google Forms (forms.new). Field type is noted after each
question — default everything else to "Paragraph" unless marked otherwise.
Keep required only on Email; leave the rest optional so people don't bail.

## FORM 1 — Fitness

**Title:** Shipyard Interview — Fitness

**Description:** For anyone who saves workout videos/Reels from Instagram, TikTok
or YouTube. ~5 min, specific recent examples over general opinions.

1. Email (for scheduling a follow-up chat) — *Short answer, Required*
2. Tell me about the last workout video/Reel you saved. — *Paragraph*
3. Where did you find it? — *Short answer*
4. Why did you save it? — *Paragraph*
5. What were you hoping to do with it when you saved it? — *Paragraph*
6. When did you actually use it? — *Short answer*
7. Walk me through exactly how you'd find it again right now. — *Paragraph*
8. What happened between finding it and actually starting the workout? — *Paragraph*
9. Roughly how many workout videos do you think you have saved? — *Multiple choice: 0–5 / 6–20 / 21–50 / 50+*
10. How do you currently organize them? — *Paragraph*
11. Have you ever known you saved a workout but couldn't find it? — *Multiple choice: Yes / No*
12. How long does it typically take to find a specific one? — *Short answer*
13. What do you do when you can't find it? — *Paragraph*
14. What information do you have to extract yourself (exercises, sets, reps, rest, equipment)? — *Paragraph*
15. Do you pause/rewind while following along? — *Multiple choice: Yes / No / Sometimes*
16. Do you write the workout into another app? — *Multiple choice: Yes / No*
17. Do you use a separate fitness app while following the video? — *Multiple choice: Yes / No*
18. Have you tried another solution for this? What worked and what didn't? — *Paragraph*
19. If Instagram had perfect search across your saved posts tomorrow, would that completely solve the problem? — *Multiple choice: Yes, completely / Mostly / No, something else is the real problem*
20. What is the most annoying part of your current workflow? — *Paragraph*
21. Open to a 15–20 min follow-up call this week? — *Multiple choice: Yes / No*
