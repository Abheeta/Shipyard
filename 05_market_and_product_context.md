# SHIPYARD — MARKET / PRODUCT CONTEXT

## Why this problem feels timely in 2025–2026

The key shift is not simply “more Reels.”

It is the convergence of:
1. Social platforms increasingly acting as discovery/search engines.
2. Massive short-form content volume.
3. People accumulating large amounts of saved useful content.
4. AI becoming capable of understanding video/audio/text and turning it into structured information.
5. Users increasingly expecting AI to organize information instead of manually categorizing it.

The thesis:

> Social media has become the discovery layer, but there is still an opportunity for a personal execution layer.

Examples:
- Instagram/TikTok/YouTube → discovery
- Product → understanding + structuring + retrieval + action

## Important caveat

“AI bookmark manager” is becoming a category. Products discussed during research include TapFold, Save AI, ReciMe and workout-specific organizers.

Therefore a differentiated product likely needs to go beyond:
- folders
- generic tags
- generic semantic search

The more interesting layer is:

> **What happens after AI understands what I saved?**

For fitness:
**Reel → workout**

For recipes:
**Reel → recipe + ingredients + shopping list**

For learning:
**video → structured lesson/notes**

For travel:
**Reel → place / itinerary item**

For shopping:
**Reel → product / wishlist**

## Current belief

The strongest opportunity may be **content → actionable object**, not content → bookmark.
